Stop no longer waits for the audio file

- Stop returns as soon as the live transcript is finished; the audio file is written in the background and the meeting page shows Saving audio until it is ready.
- The final transcript runs only when the live transcript was incomplete or not on Scribe v3; Settings can switch it to always or never.
- Scribe v2 is removed.

Recordings survive a struggling PC

- Audio is written straight to disk every two seconds while recording. A crash, a frozen PC or a failed encoder can no longer erase a meeting.
- Recording starts no extra programs. The audio file is encoded when you stop, about 25 seconds per recorded hour.
- If encoding cannot run at stop, the audio stays on disk and is finished the next time Meetily starts.
- The calendar comes only from your own Outlook connection.
